Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

These apps do not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

Scope

This privacy policy applies to all applications, games, and tools published by Mark Hunanyan (the “Apps”), on every platform they are released for — including mobile, desktop, and web. It explains what information the Apps handle — which, in short, is none.

Information We Collect

The Apps do not collect any personal information. We do not ask for, gather, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • User accounts, passwords, or login credentials
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, messages, or calendar data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage and analytics data
  • Payment or financial information

Data Stored on Your Device

Some Apps save settings, preferences, or progress so they work the way you left them. This information stays on your device only, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Third-Party Services

The Apps contain no advertising networks, no analytics or crash reporting SDKs, and no social media integrations. No third party receives data from the Apps.

The Apps are distributed through app stores and platforms such as Google Play, the Apple App Store, Steam, and itch.io. Those platforms may collect certain information as part of distributing and running apps — for example, purchase and install statistics, or crash reports you choose to send them. That data is handled by the platform, not by us, and is covered by each platform's own privacy policy, such as the Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The Apps request only the permissions required for their features to function. No permission is used to collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Data Sharing and Sale

Because no data is collected, nothing is shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to any third party for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

The Apps do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. No personal data is gathered from users of any age, so there is nothing to disclose, delete, or request under COPPA or similar regulations.

Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, export, or delete. Uninstalling an App removes any locally stored settings from your device.

Security

No personal data is transmitted or stored on any server we control, which means there is no personal data that could be exposed in a breach.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future App version introduces a feature that handles data differently — this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change. Continued use of the Apps after an update means you accept the revised policy.
